Terms & Refund Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

These terms apply to websites designed, hosted and maintained by EliMiz Studio ("we", "us") for our clients ("you").

1. Plans

  • Own it: $499 one-time setup, then $10/month for hosting, your domain and small updates. The website design and content are yours.
  • Start today: $89.99 setup, then $25/month for hosting, your domain and small updates, with a 12-month minimum term. After 12 months it continues month to month. You can switch to Own it at any time by paying the remaining balance of the $499 setup fee (the $499 minus the $89.99 you already paid).

2. Payment

Payments are processed securely by PayPal. Monthly fees are billed automatically on the same day each month. Work to put your site live and register your domain begins only after your first payment has gone through.

3. What's included

  • A custom website designed for your business.
  • Hosting and a standard domain (such as .com) registered for your business.
  • Small updates such as hours, prices, photos, menu items and text, up to about 30 minutes of work per month. Unused time does not roll over.
  • Larger changes (new pages, redesigns, new features) are quoted separately and done only with your approval.

4. Your content

You confirm you have the right to use any photos, logos and text you send us. We use original illustrations and do not copy other people's photos.

5. Refund policy

  • You see a preview of your website before paying anything.
  • If you are not happy with your site after paying, tell us within 7 days of your first payment and before the site goes live on your domain, and we'll refund your setup fee in full.
  • Once your site is live and your domain is registered, setup fees are non-refundable.
  • Monthly fees already paid are non-refundable, but you will not be charged again after cancelling (subject to the 12-month minimum on the Start today plan).

6. Cancelling

  • Own it: cancel the $10/month any time. We'll send you your website files and transfer your domain to you so you can host it elsewhere.
  • Start today: after the 12-month minimum, cancel any time. Your site will be taken offline at the end of the paid month unless you switch to Own it. If you cancel early, the remaining months of the minimum term are due.
  • If a payment fails and is not resolved within 14 days, we may take the site offline until it is.

7. Availability

We use reliable hosting and monitor your site, but we can't guarantee 100% uptime. We'll fix problems on our side as quickly as we can.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll let current clients know by email before any change that affects what they pay.
