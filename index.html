Questions

Do I really see the site before paying?

Yes. I build a preview of your website first and send you the link. You only pay if you like it and want it live.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. I take care of the hosting, the domain and the security. If you need a change, just email me.

What's a domain, and do I get one?

A domain is your web address, like yourbusiness.com. It's included in both plans and registered for your business.

How do updates work?

Email me what you'd like changed, such as new hours, prices or photos, and I'll update your site, usually within 1–2 business days.

Can I cancel?

The Own it plan's $10/month can be cancelled any time, and you keep your website files and domain. The Start today plan has a 12-month minimum, then it's month to month. Details are in the Terms.

Want a free preview of your new site?

Send me your business name and city, and I'll build your preview. No cost, no obligation.
